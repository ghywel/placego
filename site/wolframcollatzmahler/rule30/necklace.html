The catch

The ring repeats along the whole line, so it has infinitely many black cells. The prize asks about finite starts, so this is not a counterexample.

It is also rigid. In its search domain, the live states form a single 84-cycle and only one of 20 entrance paths survives, so there are no free choices and the family it gives has zero entropy. The route through this fixed six-step domain is closed; general all-S behaviour stays open.
