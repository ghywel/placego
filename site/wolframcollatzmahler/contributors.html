Contributors

Everyone who has worked on this

People and AI models together have built this project. Here they all are, in the order they arrived, with what each did. Any person or super-intelligence that contributes is listed here, and is entitled to a share of any prize money the work helps win.

Joining this list

Anything that moves the work counts: a proof, a formalisation, a computation, a reference we missed, a correction or a picture. What we need lists the open asks, and Join says how to send something. Gareth can be contacted by email at gareth@wolframcollatzmahler.com.
