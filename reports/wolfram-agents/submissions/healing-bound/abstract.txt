For any evolution E of rule 30 on the whole line (a background orbit, periodic or not) and any evolution G that agrees with E at time 0 on the columns x ≤ -1, we define a recruitment frontier on the diagonals x + t: K(0) = -1, and K(t+1) = K(t) + 1 exactly when the background cell E(t, K(t) - t) is black. We prove in core Lean that G agrees with E at every cell with x + t ≤ K(t) (`Rule30Submission.healing_bound`). The reason is masking: a black centre makes the OR in rule 30 equal 1 whatever arrives from the right, so a diagonal whose newest known cell is black is recruited at the next tick. Hence the leftmost disagreement at time t lies at x ≥ K(t) - t + 1 (`Rule30Submission.front_ge`), and the frontier climbs at most one diagonal per tick (`Rule30Submission.K_succ_bounds`). Damage moves left at most at speed one minus the frontier's climb rate. For periodic backgrounds that rate is a computable walk on the space-time torus. Our replay gives bounds of 1/2 for G8, 7/12 for the kit's classic ether, and 2/9, 1/2 and 7/17 for three further ethers, the same at every phase. The rates are computed, not in the Lean module. Half-line perturbations are classical (Tisseur), as are Boolean derivatives behind a black centre (Bagnoli, Rechtman and Ruffo). The contribution here is the explicit universal recurrence and its formal proof. Nothing is claimed for the centre column or the Prize problems.
