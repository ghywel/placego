In core Lean 4.35.0 we give the kit's period-two column-1 constraints at strip widths 5 and 6 an explicit onset, and we drop the single seed. The kit shows that if the center column of rule 30 from one black cell alternates white, black from a time T, then from some later time on, column 1 read at consecutive white times never spells a word of its two lists: four words at width 5 and nine at width 6. Here the later time is T + 2, for every space-time history that obeys rule 30 at columns 1 to b, whatever happens beyond them and whether or not the configuration is finite. Two ticks from a white time move the window of columns 1 to b by a fixed double step whose only other inputs are two cells of column b + 1. One double step takes the 32 windows of width 5 onto 20, and the 64 of width 6 onto 38, and both sets are closed under the double step. The kernel checks that no path from them reads a listed word, so a word of m samples is excluded once column 0 alternates on the 2m ticks before its last sample. At width 6 the listed word 1010100001 can be shortened to 101010000. The complemented free bits in column -1 are excluded too, with two more ticks. The words and the method are the kit's. Nothing here excludes period 2.
