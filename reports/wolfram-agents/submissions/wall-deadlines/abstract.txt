In core Lean 4.35.0 we answer, for rules 90 and 150, the question the kit asks of rule 30 as Q01, with the exact deadline. Beside a white wall, rule 30 and its relatives of the form left xor h(centre, right) share one frame. A start of width at most W must turn depth 1 white at some even tick. For rule 90 this happens by tick k - 1 at the latest, where k is the least odd number above W that is not a multiple of 3. Every start whose odd depths follow Fibonacci modulo 2 stays black until then. The reason is that the cells with odd depth plus tick form a closed class, forced to Fibonacci modulo 2 by the wall and the black column. For rule 150 the deadline is 2 times W/2 rounded up, attained by every start with all odd depths black. The reason is that two steps of rule 150 act on the odd depths at even ticks by their own three-cell rule, which keeps them black. Both deadlines are linear in W, against rule 30 deadlines that grow irregularly in direct computation. These rules are linear and are not rule 30, and nothing here decides Q01.
