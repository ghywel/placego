For period 2 of rule 30 the kit drives a right half R with the alternating center and reads u R, column 1 at the odd
times. Its gate trap, 829 windows of columns 1 to 16 closed under the double step whatever column 17 does, gives after
entry only the gaps 3 and 5 between black cells (gaps_of_entry). We prove in core Lean that after entry u R never
reads the gap words 333, 3353, 3533, 3553 or 5533 (gap_words_of_entry), hence never 33535555 (no_33535555_of_entry).
From the third black cell after entry, no two consecutive gaps are 3 (no_late_33). Each word is minimal for the trap
(minimal_paths), and by computation they are all it forbids. The kernel checks the path sets of the trap graph, 40
shared layers in 45 separate checks. The kit's open Q01Gap therefore weakens to Q01GapWords, which has to exclude only
the finitely supported left halves whose free bits, complemented, have gaps 3 or 5 and avoid the five words. It still gives CQ01
for every right half that enters the trap, and CQ01Empty. Its class of free bits has 0.132 bit of entropy per sample, against
0.256 for Q01Gap. Q01GapWords remains open.
