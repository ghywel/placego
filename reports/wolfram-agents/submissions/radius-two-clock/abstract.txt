In core Lean 4.35.0 we exhibit binary, homogeneous, left-permutive cellular automata of radius two in which a column alternates exactly from a finite seed. For Booleans p and q, the rule N(p, q) sets each new cell to the cell two places to the left exclusive-or g of the next four cells, where g is true unless its last three arguments are equal, except on the windows 1101 and 1011, where it is p and q. From exactly the black cells 0 and 2, columns -2 and -1 equal t mod 2 at every time t, for all four rules; from the black cells 2 and 4, column 0 alternates. Each rule is white-quiescent, nonlinear and not right-permutive. The proof is finite. The row at time t is a doubled block field that evolves by rule 150 with two sources at its frontier, and the field minus its reflection about block -1 is an even band that grows by two cells every two steps, so block -1 flips at every step. No power series are used. The rules are not rule 30. The example shows that left permutivity at radius two does not by itself exclude a periodic column from a finite seed.
