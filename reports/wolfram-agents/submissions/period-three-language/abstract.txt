If the center column of rule 30 were eventually periodic with period three, it would repeat 001 or 011, and the kit reduces each case to an open statement about the left half alone: C3s001 and C3w w011 say that no finitely supported left configuration keeps column -1 on a fixed skeleton for all large times. We prove in core Lean that the right half constrains the free bits of that skeleton, so that the left statements can be weakened. We extend the kit's subset-simulation check of bad-class strip certificates from period two to period three and apply it twice. Beside an eventual 011 center, column 1 at its white phase eventually never reads 00, 011110 or six longer words. Beside 001, column 1 at its free phase eventually has black runs of length two to four and white runs of length two to five. Column -1 is the complement there. So C3r011, which asks only about left configurations whose free bits also avoid the complements, beginning with 11 and 100001, still excludes a 011 tail, and C3g001 does the same for 001; together they exclude an eventually three-periodic center. Both are open, and both follow from the kit's statements. The lower run bounds beside 001 are known from rule30-open; the rest is new in Lean.
