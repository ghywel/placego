In core Lean 4.35.0 we compute a second derivative of the column of the zero-boundary left half of rule 30, exactly and for every tick. The cell at depth 1 and tick t is a Boolean function of the start at depths 1 to t + 1. For every t >= 2 and every row, flipping depths t and t - 1 in the four possible ways changes this cell by t - 1 modulo 2 in total: the same constant on every row. The proof is a derivative along anti-diagonals. A cell reads its deepest input with coefficient 1, so flipping depth t changes the cell by the negated cells two levels back, and each of those real cells flips with depth t - 1 while the white wall does not. For even t the law has two consequences. Every function with vanishing second differences in those directions, in particular every affine function, disagrees with the cell somewhere on each face of four rows. And the cell is not affine. On uniformly distributed input bits, its absolute normalised Walsh correlation with any affine function is at most 1/2, a counting consequence stated in words and checked numerically for even t <= 12. The mechanism is the classical left-permutive derivative, already in the kit for the whole line. Nothing here bears on the single seed.
