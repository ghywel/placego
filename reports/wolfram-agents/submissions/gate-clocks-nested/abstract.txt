For gates g_j, one per depth, and any seed, bit j of the edge map evolves by x_j(t+1) = x_j(t) XOR g_j(x_(j-1)(t), x_(j-2)(t)), missing bits white; with g = OR this is the right edge of Rule 30. Our earlier submission R30-20261030-7b0d12 proved that the first 2m bits have period 2^E(2m) with E(2m) <= m + Q_0(m), the kit's record-charge bound, which allows the excess E(2m) - m to reach 3 from m = 19. We prove it cannot do so before 2058: E(2m) <= m + 2 for every m < 2058, every gate family and every seed (`Rule30Submission.prefixExp_le_add_two`, `Rule30Submission.first_excess_three`). Three new laws hold for every gate: no three consecutive doublings once the period is at least 2 (`Rule30Submission.no_three`); after a pair whose second bit alone doubles, the next bit doubles exactly when the gate is quadratic and the pair's first bit has odd weight (`Rule30Submission.after01_double_iff`); and after two doublings from period M, the next bit's difference order is at least M - 2 (`Rule30Submission.after11_order`). Choosing the stretch after the last visit to excess 1 before excess 3 first appears, these force two countdowns, of the first bits' order and then of the last bits' resource, which cost at least 2058 pairs together. A computed family, found by search, attains excess 2 from m = 3. Nothing is claimed about excess 3 being reached, about later thresholds or about the centre column.
