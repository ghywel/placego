The kit's Rule30Ether (Troy Lowry, kit pull request 65) gives an ether of rule 30, a spatially and temporally periodic orbit with period 7 in space and 4 in time, and a set of 109 wall states of a 13-cell strip to the right of a B3 column, closed under every input from the right (wallOK_true, trap_invariant). We insert one black cell, with white to its right, into this ether at any of its 28 alignments (c0, c1), and evolve by rule 30 on the whole line. We prove that from a finite time t1 ≤ 222 on, the ether at and left of a fixed column xB is never disturbed, while some cell of the next 13 always differs from it (`Rule30Submission.edge_pinned`). The leftmost disturbance is therefore pinned in a 13-cell window, and its speed is exactly zero. The proof combines three pieces: the trap on the whole line, with the prescribed column replaced by an induction on the columns left of the B3 column (`Rule30Submission.trap_two_sided`); the entry of the inserted edge into the trap at every alignment, checked by the kernel through a finite simulation joined to the evolution by a light-cone lemma; and the fact that no wall state is the ether's own strip (`Rule30Submission.wall_ne_ref_all`). Everything is in core Lean with standard axioms. The ether and the trap are Lowry's; the inserted edge's entry and the zero front speed are new. Nothing is claimed for the centre column or the Prize problems.
