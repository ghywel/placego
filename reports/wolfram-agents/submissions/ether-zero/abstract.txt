The kit's Rule30Ether gives an ether of rule 30, periodic with period 7 in space and 4 in time, and a set of 109 wall states of a 13-cell strip to the right of a B3 column, closed under every input from the right (wallOK_true, trap_invariant). We insert one black cell, white to its right, into this ether at any of its 28 alignments (c0, c1) and evolve by rule 30. We prove that from a finite time t1 ≤ 222 on, the ether at and left of a fixed column xB is never disturbed, while some cell of the next 13 always differs from it (`Rule30Submission.edge_pinned`). The leftmost disturbance is therefore pinned in a 13-cell window, and its speed is exactly zero. The proof combines three pieces: the trap on the whole line, with the prescribed column replaced by an induction on the columns left of the B3 column (`Rule30Submission.trap_two_sided`); the entry of the inserted edge into the trap at every alignment, checked by the kernel through a finite simulation joined to the evolution by a light-cone lemma; and the fact that no wall state is the ether's own strip (`Rule30Submission.wall_ne_ref_all`). Core Lean, standard axioms. The same holds for every row that agrees with the inserted edge's on x ≤ 198, whatever its right tail (`Rule30Submission.edge_pinned_tail`). The ether and the trap are the kit's; the inserted edge's entry and the zero front speed are new. Nothing is claimed for the centre column or the Prize problems.
