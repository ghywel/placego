For every configuration $x : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathrm{Bool}$ whose center column reads the clock, $\mathrm{evol}\,x\,t\,0 = [t \text{ odd}]$ for all $t$, the first $n$ visible bits $\mathrm{visFrom}\,x\,0\,n = [\mathrm{evol}\,x\,0\,1, \mathrm{evol}\,x\,2\,1, \ldots]$ (column $1$ at the even times) lie in one set $S$ of words with $|S| \cdot 10^{9n} \le 218 \cdot 1195924139^{\,n}$ (`Rule30Submission.channel_bound_12`; the same for a clock from any even time on, `channel_bound_12_eventually`): about $0.258$ bits of information per visible bit asymptotically ($\log_2(1195924139/10^9) \approx 0.25813$, with a finite correction $\log_2 218 / n$). The bound is generic in a certificate, `Cert.bound`: a graph of automaton states with Collatz-Wielandt weights, for any window width.

The catalog reduces period 2 to `Rule30ZeroBoundary.Q01` and kernel-checks it up to width 52; it has nothing about the right neighbour of a clocked column. Our submissions R30-20261022-91b0b0 and R30-20261023-8084e6 excluded particular right neighbours; this one bounds all of them at once.

The $M$ cells right of the center form a window; under the clock, two steps map a window row to `step2` with the cell beyond the window at the two successive times as free inputs. The kernel rebuilds each automaton state as a bitset of $2^M$ bits from the graph alone, with GMP bit operations, checks that every edge is closed (`check_sound`) and that the weights satisfy $D(w_{j_0} + w_{j_1}) \le R\, w_i$ (`weight_bound`); `win_step2` ties the window to the kit's `evol`.

Nothing here decides `Q01`: it measures how constrained the right neighbour is, not that no configuration keeps the clock. Width 12 is proved; widths 14 and 16 (about 0.2445 and 0.2131 bits) have the same certificate form and are not in this module.
