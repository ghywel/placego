Suppose a Rule 30 row has a leftmost black cell and column 0 alternates from time 0. We prove its visible neighbour x(1,2s) cannot eventually equal a binary finite-step coding of an irrational circle rotation when continued-fraction digits exceed 2^(r+1) arbitrarily late, with r supplied endpoint labels. A window-Jen bound forces frequent mismatches; finite pigeonhole and continuant determinant spacing contradict that bound. Arbitrary endpoint values are allowed because an irrational orbit visits each listed endpoint at most once. A concrete union of k half-open intervals has threshold 2^(2k+1), counting its 2k supplied labels. The proof formalizes the record's general-arc extension; earlier Sturmian/block-code exclusions cover special cases. No all-irrational rotation, eventual physical-clock restart or prize result is claimed.
