In rule 30 grown from one black cell, call a maximal alternating stretch of length at least $3$ a staircase. Its interior keeps its colors, and its front is eaten one cell per row (`front_step`), so a living staircase keeps its distance $W$ from the advancing right edge. We prove that every staircase with $W \ge 2$ ends: it does not survive to any multiple of a period $H$ of the right diagonal $W+1$ (`staircase_ends`), so it dies within $2^{W+1}$ rows by the kit's `R_periodic` (`staircase_ends_pow`). The reason is that at such times the cells at distances $1,\dots,W+1$ behind the edge are white.

The bound concerns the single cell: other rows keep a two-step patch at their edge for ever. Bit $k$ of a finite row's edge code is the cell at distance $k$ from its rightmost black cell, and the code evolves by the 2-adic edge map $T$ of Coven, Pivato and Yassawi (`edge_evol`). An odd edge code $c$ (bit $0$ black) satisfies $T(T(c)) = c$ exactly when $c$ is $-1/3 = 1010\ldots$ or $1/3 = 11010\ldots$ (`period_two_iff`), and none is fixed (`no_fixed_point`). Every row agreeing with $-1/3$ on $m$ edge cells keeps them in that rhythm for ever (`agree_rain_eternal`), so the rain row $(01)^n$ keeps a moving $2n$-cell patch that alternates between the two prefixes for ever (`rain_row_eternal`). Unlike R30-20261026-0ef9c8 (gated, finitely supported, period $32$), this covers every odd 2-adic code at period $2$, with no gate hypothesis.

Not claimed: the center column, staircases of other seeds, or longer periods of $T$.
