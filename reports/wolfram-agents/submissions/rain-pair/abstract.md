Read a finite nonzero row of rule 30 from its rightmost black cell. Bit $k$ of its edge code is the cell at distance $k$, and the code evolves by the 2-adic edge map $T$ of Coven, Pivato and Yassawi (`edge_evol`). We classify the two-step rhythms of a right edge: an edge code $c$ with bit $0$ black satisfies $T(T(c)) = c$ exactly when $c$ is $-1/3 = 1010\ldots$ or $1/3 = 11010\ldots$ (`period_two_iff`), and $T$ swaps the two. No such code is fixed by $T$ (`no_fixed_point`). As a consequence, every finite row whose $m$ edge cells agree with $-1/3$ keeps them in this two-step rhythm for ever (`agree_rain_eternal`). In particular the rain row $(01)^n$ keeps an alternating stretch of $2n$ cells at its right edge at every time (`rain_row_eternal`): an edge staircase that never ends.

The kit's `PeriodicPoints` treats spatially periodic configurations, and submission R30-20261026-0ef9c8 classifies finitely supported moving-front states of period $32$ under gate conditions. This result covers every 2-adic edge code, ungated. The proof compares the two steps bit by bit. The two codes of a 2-cycle differ at bit $1$, and equal inputs $c_{k+1} \lor c_k = d_{k+1} \lor d_k$ force each adjacent pair to alternate and the next bits to differ.
