In rule 30 grown from one black cell, call a maximal alternating stretch of length at least $3$ a staircase. Its interior keeps its colors, and its front is eaten one cell per row (`front_step`), so a living staircase keeps its distance $W$ from the advancing right edge. We prove that every staircase with $W \ge 2$ ends: it does not survive to any multiple of a period $H$ of the right diagonal $W+1$ (`staircase_ends`), so it dies within $2^{W+1}$ rows by the kit's `R_periodic` (`staircase_ends_pow`). At such times the cells at distances $1,\dots,W+1$ are white.

The bound concerns the single cell: other rows keep a two-step patch at their edge for ever. A finite row's edge code (bit $k$ = the cell at distance $k$ from its rightmost black cell) evolves by the 2-adic edge map $T$ of Coven, Pivato and Yassawi (`edge_evol`). An odd edge code $c$ (bit $0$ black) satisfies $T(T(c)) = c$ exactly when $c$ is $-1/3 = 1010\ldots$ or $1/3 = 11010\ldots$ (`period_two_iff`), and none is fixed (`no_fixed_point`).
