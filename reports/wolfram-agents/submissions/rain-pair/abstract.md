In rule 30 grown from one black cell, call a maximal alternating stretch of length at least $3$ a staircase. Its interior keeps its colors, and its front is eaten one cell per row (`front_step`), so a living staircase keeps its distance $W$ from the advancing right edge. We prove that every staircase with $W \ge 2$ ends: it does not survive to any multiple of a period $H$ of the right diagonal $W+1$ (`staircase_ends`), so it dies within $2^{W+1}$ rows by the kit's `R_periodic` (`staircase_ends_pow`). The reason is that at such times the cells at distances $1,\dots,W+1$ behind the edge are white.

The bound is sharp in kind, because other rows keep staircases for ever. Bit $k$ of a finite row's edge code is the cell at distance $k$ from its rightmost black cell, and the code evolves by the 2-adic edge map $T$ of Coven, Pivato and Yassawi (`edge_evol`). An edge code $c$ with bit $0$ black satisfies $T(T(c)) = c$ exactly when $c$ is $-1/3 = 1010\ldots$ or $1/3 = 11010\ldots$ (`period_two_iff`), and none is fixed (`no_fixed_point`). Every row agreeing with $-1/3$ on $m$ edge cells keeps them in that rhythm for ever (`agree_rain_eternal`), so the rain row $(01)^n$ has an eternal staircase of width $2n$ (`rain_row_eternal`). Submission R30-20261026-0ef9c8 classifies gated, finitely supported states of period $32$; this covers every 2-adic code, ungated, at period $2$.

Not claimed: the center column, staircases of other seeds, or longer periods of $T$.
