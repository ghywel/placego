In core Lean 4.35.0, with the kit's own definitions, we prove that the kit's AbsorptionT t001 is false. The kit reduces the word 001 of least period three to AbsorptionT t001: every finitely supported right half driven by 001, from any phase, eventually has its first 47 cells in a state of the 815-state trap t001 at a label of the same phase. The kit lists it as open. A second checked trap for 001 refutes it. It has period 15, 52 cells and 153 states; no state of it, cut to 47 cells, is a state of t001 at the same phase, and the right half given by its state 0 stays in it for ever by the kit's trap invariant, so it never enters t001. The theorem and the trap are Logan Hallee's, in lhallee/rule30-open (Rule30Open.Columns.Traps001), which proves more: entry into one of any three of four pairwise apart traps fails. This module restates the first theorem in the kit's PTrap form so that the kit's status table can be corrected; the half that rule30-open names is the trap's state 25. The kit's implication from AbsorptionT t001 to the exclusion of 001 stays true, but its hypothesis is false. Whether the center column can be eventually 001 remains open.
