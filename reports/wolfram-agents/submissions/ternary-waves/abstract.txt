In core Lean 4.35.0 we extend the kit's travelling-wave codes of rule 30 from two tiles to three, and from moving period 12 to moving period 15. In the frame that moves one cell right per step, a row is a travelling return of period P when P updates of rule 30 bring it back moved P cells to the right. The kit shows that two 34-cell tiles at period 12 join freely, so that every bi-infinite binary code gives such a row. We give three 34-cell tiles at period 12, the kit's two and one more, and three 31-cell tiles at period 15. In each family every pair of tiles joins at every offset and phase: 3672 and 4185 local rule 30 identities, checked by the Lean kernel. So every bi-infinite ternary code gives a row of exact minimal moving return 12, or 15, and two cells of each block read the code back. Hence there are 3 to the power k distinct marked rings of size 34k, or 31k, and at least 3 to the power floor(n/34), or floor(n/31), distinct words of length n in such rows: at least log2(3)/34 and log2(3)/31 bits per cell. The development is generic in the tile length, the period and the profile data, and is proved once. Nothing is claimed about other periods, about whether 15 is the next period with positive spatial entropy, or about the exact spatial entropy at either period.
