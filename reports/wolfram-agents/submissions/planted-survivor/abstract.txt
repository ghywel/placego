In core Lean 4.35.0 we construct a different map with an immortal finite row whose survival counts match arbitrarily large finite portions of rule 30's zero-boundary survival table. For L >= 1 a tree automorphism psi of the rows flips the cell at each odd depth k >= 2L + 1 at which the row agrees below k with the stripe, the row black exactly at the odd depths. It sends the stripe to the finite row trunc(2L - 1), keeps finite rows finite, and its inverse does not increase the width of any finite row except trunc(2L - 1). Conjugating the kit's step by psi gives a map T of finite rows. T fixes the empty row and fixes trunc(2L - 1), whose depth 1 is therefore black at every time. For every width bound W and time t, the rows of width at most W that pass every even check of depth 1 through t number the same under T as under rule 30, plus one exactly when 2L - 1 <= W and 2 ceil(W/2) <= t. The immortal row takes the place of the truncated stripe that dies at time 2 ceil(W/2). T is not a cellular automaton and not rule 30, and nothing here decides Q01.
