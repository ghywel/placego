In core Lean 4.35.0 we fix the algebraic normal form of the first check of the zero-boundary left half of rule 30 for every n. Keep column -1 black at every even step and let its values at the odd steps be n free bits. The first check past the free range is the cell at depth 2n + 1, the kit's Rule30FirstChecks.check. For n >= 1 its top coefficient is n mod 2, and for n >= 2 the coefficient of the monomial without the second free bit is n + 1 mod 2, so the check has degree n for odd n and n - 1 for even n. With the kit's first_check_count, the number of rows of width at most 2n that are black at column -1 at every even tick through 2n is congruent to n modulo 2. The Walsh sum is 2 mod 4 exactly at odd n >= 2, and 0 at n = 1. In the other clock phase, with the free bits at the even steps, the check has odd weight for every n. The proof is a derivative along the anti-diagonals of the reconstruction. Nothing here bears on the single seed.
