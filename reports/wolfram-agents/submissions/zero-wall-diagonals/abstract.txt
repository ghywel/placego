In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove what a counterexample to the kit question Q01 would have to look like. A finite start of the zero-boundary left half of rule 30 that survives every even check is a finitely supported constrained row, and its values at the odd steps form its free word f. For such a row, for every K, the number of distinct length-n factors of f exceeds n + K for all large n; f cannot have the bounded complexity excess of Sturmian words. The abstract word lemmas and the diagonal theory for nonzero finite rows are unconditional. For every nonzero finite row with last black cell at depth m >= 1, the diagonals measured from the moving edge obey the left-diagonal recurrence whenever j + 2 <= m + t, equality included. From this we port the kit single-seed diagonal theory with entry-time guards: eventual periods 2 to the power j, a finite profile bound, and eventually black diagonals of arbitrarily large index. These force bounded lag for bounded-slack repeats of the free word. A word argument with explicit right-special witnesses then turns bounded lag into unbounded complexity excess. The free-word theorems are conditional: such rows exist only if Q01 fails. Nothing here decides Q01.
