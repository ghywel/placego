In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove that any five adjacent physical columns of any Rule 30 trajectory contain at most 3T + 2 black cells during any interval of T ticks. There is no assumption on the initial configuration or the starting time. For a five-cell word u, let H(u) be the maximum of zero and two minus the black population of its four rightmost cells. A finite Boolean check proves pop(u) - 3 <= H(v) - H(u) whenever v is the next window, with both exterior input cells arbitrary. Summing gives the stronger endpoint bound C5 <= 3T + H(final) - H(initial), and H lies between zero and two. Consequently the same window contains at least 2T - 2 white cells. Both constants are sharp: an all-black window requires the additive two at T = 1, and the fixed alternating row has three black cells in a suitable five-column window at every tick. We specialize the bound to the kit's arbitrary-row evolution and official singleton diagram. The result controls a sum over five columns; it gives no upper-density bound for an individual column, no positive black-density lower bound, and no solution of a prize problem.
