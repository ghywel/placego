For period two of rule 30 the kit's coupled question CQ01 asks that, for every finite right half R driven by the alternating center column, the sideways row Psi R has infinitely many black cells; the kit proves this whenever column 1 of R at the white ticks is eventually periodic. We prove in core Lean that three closed locks force such periodicity. A lock is a set of windows of the columns 1 to W, in p classes, such that every double step from a window of one class, whatever the two cells beyond the window, lands in the next class, and the classes fix column 1. The kernel checks a lock of width 34 with 25 windows and column word 10000, a lock of width 30 with 66 windows and word 0101000, and a lock of width 45 with 33 windows and word 0010100. Every right half whose window enters one of them, finite or not, satisfies CQ01. The right halves 28998, 1891 and 3793493 enter at ticks 15, 27 and 29, and so does every right half that shares their first 49, 57 or 74 cells. The period-five lock lies inside the kit's gate trap, where CQ01 had been conditional on Q01Gap. The period-seven lock of width 30 is, in a slightly different model, the SevenLock of rule30-open. Nothing here decides CQ01 or CQ01Empty.
