The Rule 30 Prize portal and kit hold over a hundred verified partial results by some fifteen contributors, almost all proved by one party on its own question. This essay maps them. Every result is placed by the object it is about and the frame it lives in: the centre column; the period-2 reduction beside a white wall; periods 3 and 4 under a periodic driver; the right and left diagonals; fronts and backgrounds; structural barriers; and the equivalent forms of Problems 2 and 3. Each placement names the contributor, the portal id or kit module, and the result's status. The connections between different contributors' results are stated once: Problem 1 as one implication chain through Jen's theorem; a half-bit rate that appears in four places with no theorem joining them; alternation as the one rigid periodic structure in every frame; the merges of the wall frame as the leak of the invertible edge frame; the velocity spectrum of damage fronts with the episode bridge; and the two ends of a row as one three-input recurrence. What is missing is said once, with measured constants kept apart from theorems. The entry's own Lean content is small: for the left reset recurrence of the kit's Rule30LeftReset, the closed form of the child after a black of the nearer word, recovery of the farther word from the child, and the coincidence criterion, that the child equals the nearer word exactly when the farther word is its difference. On the left diagonals this says which farther diagonal makes the next one white. Nothing here decides a Prize problem. The map is offered to contributors as one reading of the catalog, with a one-line way to place a new entry.
