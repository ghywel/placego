For every real slope alpha, phase theta and natural C, the mechanical word floor(theta+(s+1)alpha)-floor(theta+s alpha) has a repetition of positive lag q on [a,b] with b > 2a+q+C and b+q <= 251(C+4). Every recoding by a block map of width w+1 has one by 251(C+4)+250w. Suppose a Rule 30 row is white left of -L and column 0 alternates white and black from time 0. Then any stretch [a,b] of the visible neighbour x(1,2s) that agrees with such a recoded word has b-a < 251(L+2a+4)+250w, so reset times obey t' <= 503t+251L+1004+250w. The qualitative obstruction is classical, and finite continued-fraction estimates exist (Bugeaud-Kim); we claim neither priority nor sharpness. This is a checked quantitative bound with explicit Rule 30 endpoints. No density, realizability or prize result is claimed.
