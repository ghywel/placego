The rows of width at most 2n that keep column -1 of the zero-boundary left half black at every even tick below 2n correspond exactly to the free words of length n. The next check, at tick 2n, is the first that can fail, and it reads one cell of the forced row. This module computes that check in two exact ways. The rows that pass number 2^n minus the words on which the cell is black. And the cell differs from the cell of the shifted word on exactly 2^m + ones m of the words of length m + 2, where ones m counts the black cells two places back. With the elementary bounds on two overlapping sets, this pair law gives two linear inequalities between the checks. So among any three consecutive n from 2 on, at most one check is near constant: for at least two of them the share of rows that pass lies between 1/40 and 39/40. This bounds the first check only. From the second check on the fresh bits are pinned, so the bounds do not multiply. Nothing here proves Q01, and Problems 1 to 3 stay open.
