In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove a strengthened ranking barrier for the zero-boundary left half of rule 30, where the kit poses its period-2 question Q01. Fix a margin u(W) tending to infinity, and call a finite row of width W late if its double step survives and column -1 stays black at every even time up to W - u(W). Consider ranks made of an arbitrary integer function of the width and two boundary windows plus an integer density summed over the m-cell windows of the row. If such a rank is at least -KW on late rows and never increases along their double steps, the density is conserved on every closed walk, hence a constant plus a shift coboundary, and the rank depends only on the width and the boundary windows. So no rank of this form strictly decreases along every late double step, extending the kit's barrier B2 to the late population and to additive ranks. The proof plants a periodic word in the free middle of a row behind a stripe prefix and uses the balance of the four-step image. Popcount minus W^2 shows that the lower bound is needed. Nothing here decides Q01 or Problem 1.
