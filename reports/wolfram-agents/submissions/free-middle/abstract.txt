In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove a strengthened ranking barrier for the zero-boundary left half of rule 30. Fix a margin u(W) tending to infinity. A finite row of width W is late if it passes the checks at times 0 and 2 and every even check through max(W - u(W), 0). Consider a rank consisting of an arbitrary integer function of the width and two fixed boundary windows plus an integer density on m-cell windows. Here m and the edge-window width are positive; for the boundary reduction, both windows have at least m - 1 cells. If the rank is at least -KW on late rows and never increases along their double steps, the density is conserved on every closed walk, hence a constant plus a shift coboundary, and the rank depends only on the width and boundary windows. Consequently no nonnegative rank of this form strictly decreases along every late double step. The proof plants a periodic word behind a stripe prefix and uses balance of the four-step image. The available middle grows with both width and margin; no sublinear assumption on u is needed. Popcount minus W^2 shows why the linear lower bound matters. Nothing here decides Q01 or Problem 1.
