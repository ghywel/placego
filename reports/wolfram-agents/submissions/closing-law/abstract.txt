Read in the frame that moves with it, the right edge of Rule 30 is a gate clock: bit j is XORed with the OR of bits j - 1 and j - 2, and the first n bits are periodic in time with period 2^E(n). Adding one bit either doubles that period or keeps it, which gives a word of doubling letters. We prove in core Lean, for the kit's gate clocks with any gates that have a quadratic term (OR included) and any seed, an exact countdown: one non-doubling step lowers the kit's difference order by exactly one, not merely by at most one. From it follows the closing law. After a doubling at period q0, a run of m pairs "double, keep" followed by a doubling has m + 1 <= q0, and the next letter is a doubling exactly when m + 1 = q0. Hence a closing run has length one less than a power of two, the word 1101011 never occurs, 11011 closes with a doubling only when q0 = 2, and once the period is at least 4 any five consecutive letters contain at most three doublings. The kit's checker passes with standard axioms only. The essay computes the letters of the single black cell and checks every run against the law.
