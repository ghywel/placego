Read a Rule 30 row from its rightmost black cell, as the kit's Rule30FrozenRuler does, and let a phase be a family of depth columns obeying the edge map at depths two and up with a time period, as in our entry R30-20261103-d37d24. We prove in core Lean, with standard axioms only, that three periodic phases of our record cannot be followed by any black-edge code from a bounded depth on. The longest episode at depths 14 and 15 of the period-16 phase G16, a 128-cell ring with lag 72, is 22 ticks; at depths 8 and 9 of the period-1024 phase P1024 it is 24; at depths 10 and 11 of the period-32 phase on a 480-cell ring it is 24; each at every alignment. Hence no black-edge code, periodic or not, follows any of them at every depth from some depth at most 14, 8 or 10 on. The certificate is a tree of top words: an episode of t ticks depends only on the top 2t bits of the prefix, by locality; the tree extends alive words by two fresh bits per tick, prunes at a mismatch, and is empty at ticks 23, 25 and 25. The kernel checks about 83,000, 71,000 and 140,000 candidate tests. Controls: each longest episode reproduces the record's computed table, and shortening the tree by one tick fails at a known maximizer. Nothing here concerns other depths, the uniform exclusion of these phases, the centre column or the Prize problems.
