In the zero-boundary left half of rule 30, Q01 says that no finitely supported start keeps the cell beside the wall black at every even tick. The kit states two open hypotheses on streams over Fin 4, the constant-tail separator SEP and the binary wedge hypothesis BWH, and records no implication between them and Q01. We prove in core Lean where they sit. A chart sends each constrained start Theta f to an endpoint stream whose inverse cone is the start's own run, and that stream is hard-core exactly when the free bits f have no two adjacent white bits. So SEP is equivalent to Q01 restricted to those free bits, and Q01 is SEP together with the free bits that meet two adjacent whites infinitely often. SEP implies Q01Gap and CQ01Empty. It is also the statement that no width-one layer beside the alternating column, with any input from column 2, has a finite forced left row. BWH is equivalent to exact-width death bounds, so under BWH every start of width at most W ≥ 16 dies by tick 2W + 4, and BWH implies Q01. Kernel-checked controls show that neither guard of those bounds can be dropped. Nothing here proves SEP, BWH or Q01.
