In core Lean 4.35.0 we answer, for a linear relative of rule 30, the question the kit asks of rule 30 as Q01. Rows are finite, at depths 1, 2 and so on next to a white wall, and the hybrid applies rule 150 at odd depths and rule 90 at even depths. A row is immortal when its depth-1 cell is black at every even time. A finite row is immortal exactly when x1 = 1 and, for every j, x(4j+3) = x(4j+5) and x(4j+2) = x(4j+1) + x(4j+3); in powers of A = S + 1/S these are the rows (A + A²)p(A⁴) + r(A⁴) with p(0) = 1 and r(0) = 0. For width at most W >= 2 the immortal rows are in bijection with the binary words of length W/2 - 1, and the kernel confirms that they number 2 to the power W/2 - 1 for W <= 12. The column of every nonzero finite row is not eventually periodic. The proof rests on a decimation D with D P² = P D for the pair map P of the hybrid, so that the even-time column is a constant plus the column of the decimated row. The hybrid is not rule 30, and nothing here decides Q01.
