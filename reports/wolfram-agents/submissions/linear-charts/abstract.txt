In core Lean 4.35.0 we prove that the kit's free-bit chart is the only way, up to an affine map, to read the constrained starts of the zero-boundary left half of rule 30 as an affine space on which two steps of the dynamics act affinely. The constrained starts are those that are white at depth 0 and black at depth 1 at every even tick. The kit's Theta and the free-bit read-off are inverse bijections between them and all bit sequences, and turn two steps into the one-sided shift. Let A be an affine subspace of the bit sequences over the two-element field, Lm affine on A, and Phi a bijection from the constrained starts onto A, finitely dependent in both directions, with Phi of two steps equal to Lm of Phi. Then Phi composed with Theta is affine. The proof pulls the affine structure back to the free bits and proves a lemma: every commutative operation on one-sided bit sequences that has the white sequence as neutral element, satisfies (x + y) + y = x, commutes with the shift and has bit 0 determined by a finite window, is pointwise xor. Continuity is represented as uniform finite dependence, and its equivalence with continuity on compact domains is not formalized. The automorphism theorem of the one-sided 2-shift is due to Hedlund, and the lemma is probably known. Nothing here decides Q01 or period 2.
