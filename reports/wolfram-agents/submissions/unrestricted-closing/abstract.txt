In core Lean 4.35.0 we show that an unrestricted closing property fails on the kit's constrained class of the zero-boundary left half of rule 30: the rows whose depth 1 is black at every even tick. Write G for two ticks. Take the row A that repeats 1011111 from depth 1, the row B that is 11 followed by repeats of 01001011001000, and the row C that repeats 1100001. We prove that G A = C, G B = C and G C = A at every depth. So all three rows stay in the class. Their free words, the cells at depth 1 at the odd ticks, are 10 repeated, 00 followed by 10 repeated, and 01 repeated, respectively, and the kit's Theta recovers each row from its free word. A and B differ at every depth 14q + 3, and one tick later at every depth 14q + 4. Hence two rows of the class with the same row after one double step need not agree on any tail of depths: eventual-tail closing on the class fails when nothing is assumed about the gate streams. The guarded closing properties of open kit pull request 52, which assume legal gate streams, are not contradicted, since the common future here leaves their legal windows at once. Nothing here decides Q01 or period 2.
