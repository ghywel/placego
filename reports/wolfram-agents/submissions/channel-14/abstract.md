For every configuration $x : \mathbb{Z} \to \mathrm{Bool}$ whose center column reads the clock, $\mathrm{evol}\,x\,t\,0 = [t \text{ odd}]$ for all $t$, the first $n$ visible bits $\mathrm{visFrom}\,x\,0\,n$ (column $1$ at the even times) lie in one set $S$ of words with $|S| \cdot 10^{9n} \le 300 \cdot 1184655442^{\,n}$ (`Rule30Submission.channel_bound_14`): about $0.2445$ bits of information per visible bit asymptotically ($\log_2(1184655442/10^9) \approx 0.24447$, with a finite correction $\log_2 300 / n$).

The catalog reduces period 2 to `Rule30ZeroBoundary.Q01` and has nothing about the right neighbour of a clocked column. Our submission R30-20261023-4767e0 proved this bound at width $12$ ($0.258$ bits) by rebuilding the window automaton inside one kernel computation; that does not scale, since the kernel's caches live for one declaration. Here the certificate is explicit: the bitset of each of the $919$ states of the width-$14$ automaton, its graph and Collatz-Wielandt weights.

One theorem per state (`s14_i`) has the kernel sweep the state's bitset and check that every member's four two-step images, with the cell beyond the window at the two successive times as free inputs, lie in the successor of its label (`stateCheck`); `explicit_sound` assembles the $919$ theorems into the closure of every edge, `bound_of` and `weight_bound` give the bound, and `win_step2` ties the window to the kit's `evol`. The sets are checked data, not derived: any family with the root full and every edge closed bounds the words.

Nothing here decides `Q01`: the bound measures the right neighbour. Width $16$ (about $0.2131$ bits) would need about $45$ MB of state literals in this form and is not attempted.
