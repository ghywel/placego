For every irrational slope $0 \le \alpha < 1$ and every intercept $\theta$, the mechanical word $m_{\alpha,\theta}(i) = \lfloor \theta + (i+1)\alpha \rfloor - \lfloor \theta + i\alpha \rfloor$ is not the sequence of visible bits of any configuration with a leftmost black cell $L \ge 1$ cells left of the center whose center column reads the clock $0101\ldots$ from some time $T_0$ on: $\mathrm{evol}\,x_0\,(2i)\,1 = m_{\alpha,\theta}(i)$ for all $i$ is impossible (`Rule30Submission.sturmian_excluded`; the clock from time $0$ without the condition on $L$, `sturmian_excluded_clock0`; the fractional-part form $m(i) = [1 - \alpha \le \{\theta + i\alpha\}]$, `sturmian_excluded_fract`). The same holds for every block code of the word (`sturmian_block_excluded`).

The catalog reduces period 2 to `Rule30ZeroBoundary.Q01` and says nothing about the right neighbour of a clocked center column. Our submission R30-20261023-e872a3 excluded the characteristic words, intercept $0$, through the standard words; this essay removes that restriction with a combinatorial argument.

A Sturmian word has at most $n + 1$ distinct blocks of length $n$, so two of the $n + 2$ blocks starting at positions $0, \ldots, n + 1$ coincide (`exists_block_collision`, by the pigeonhole principle on threshold sets); irrationality keeps the two positions far apart as $n$ grows (`no_small_period`), which gives near-squares with slack $1$ at unbounded scales (`sturmian_pairs`). Shifting the intercept by $T_0 \alpha$ moves the clock's start to time $0$, and Corollary F (R30-20261022-91b0b0) forbids such near-squares.

Nothing is claimed for rational slopes, nor for any right neighbour that is not a block code of a Sturmian word.
