Every nonzero finite Rule 30 row y has a unique finite root x and age n with F^n(x)=y, where 2n does not exceed the distance between y's extreme black cells (`Rule30Submission.finite_root_age`). For a target with endpoints L,R, an actual predecessor p that is white at every site i>=R is finite exactly when y(L+1)=p(L+1)=1 (`finite_parent_test`). Normalized n-step evolution is a bijection from width-w words onto width-(w+2n) words with an n-step finite past. Among normalized width-W words, exactly 2^(W-4) have a finite predecessor for W>=4; the counts at widths 1,2,3 are 0,0,1.

The contribution is an explicit physical-row formal interface for known finite-ancestry structure, combining a boundary test, simultaneous root-age uniqueness and exhaustive duplicate-free counting. The kit already provides right-asymptotic injectivity and inverse reconstruction in Preimages, and edge facts in FiniteConfigs and FrozenRuler. Karttunen's 2016 OEIS records already describe the finite ancestor and age; no mathematical priority is claimed. The proof uses endpoint growth, right-to-left reconstruction and an exact translated-image bijection. It gives no new centre-column exclusion, infinite-past classification or orbit-frequency law.
