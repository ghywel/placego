Read a row of rule 30 from its rightmost black cell, so that one tick is the kit's edge map T, and take rows whose cells alternate from some index on. Their wall is the last equal pair below that tail. Each row has exactly one predecessor. Going backward, a wall that rises three cells on three ticks running shows one of two eleven-cell letters, A or B (deep_words), and BB and AAA never occur (no_BB, no_AAA). A set of windows closed under the backward tick for every pair of lower cells holds a row for ever, whatever lies below it (held_forever). We give three such sets. Four windows of 45 cells force the letters AB repeated, six of 136 force AAB repeated, and forty of 175 force them with no reset word at a fixed depth below the wall (LockAB.letters, LockAAB.letters, LockThird.letters, LockThird.no_floor). A closed set of 798 windows of 45 cells never reaches the first lock and bounds alternation at 17 ticks (Cloud.avoids_lock, Cloud.no_long_alternation), and one explicit row passes from that set into the third lock (Entry.entry). No row in these sets is periodic under T. The kernel checks everything in about 13 seconds on an M5. Whether every row with such a tail reaches a letter, or a lock, stays open. Nothing here concerns the center column or the Prize problems.
