In core Lean 4.35.0 we strengthen two of the kit's barriers for proofs that the center column of rule 30 is not eventually 2-periodic. A ranking proof would give every finite row of the zero-boundary left half a rank that falls at every surviving double step. The kit shows that no natural-number rank of the width and two bounded windows can do this, and that no finite automaton reading the row from its edge can supply the rank as its longest accepted prefix. We let the rank depend also on the image of the whole row under any morphism from Boolean words into any finite monoid, with values in any well-founded order. No such rank exists. This holds with the row read from its edge or from the wall, and in the kit's coupled two-sided frame at every flip of the center column. The kit's two-window barriers are the cases of the one-element monoid. The witnesses are the kit's: truncated stripes, whose rows after j double steps are a fixed word followed by a run of 01 pairs, and truncations of the 7-periodic ether. Separately, the rows 1493 and 1507, of width 11, survive fourteen double steps. So no integer rank between -1 and the width falls at every surviving step, and the kit's automaton barrier holds without its bound on the number of states. These are statements about proof shapes. Nothing here decides Q01 or period 2.
