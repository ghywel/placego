## What the proof says

For any actual Rule30 orbit, a black current cell fixes its next color from its left neighbor, independently
of its right neighbor: a XOR (1 OR b)=NOT a. Thus two valid evolutions sharing that black cell and its left
neighbor cannot depart at the next tick. A wheel departure, while the preceding wheel state and left clock
still agree, therefore has a WHITE predecessor. This is the familiar rule identity, not a new masking law.

The remaining two kick constraints in PROOFS28 are phase bookkeeping: with old and new phases both even,
the time-to-angle multiplier17 modulo56 preserves parity, and a departing new bit has the opposite color
to the old predicted bit. In the supplied wheel W, the even black angles are exactly44,46,...,54. Hence a
