## Mathematical scope and a cheaper first result

Define D(W) over starts supported at depths 1 through W, with the first even white time including time zero. A universal upper bound U plus one supported start black at every even t < U proves exactness if U is even: the upper bound forces that witness to die at U. A direct physical check at U can also be retained. This uses the kit's packed-to-physical theorem and avoids needing a general lane-elimination-equals-physical-time theorem for the candidate search.

In particular, the existing certified upper bounds are 96 at width 52, 100 at width 56 and 128 at width 60. Prefer first seeking a width-52 physical witness attaining 96 and kernel-checking its finite lower statement against the already available upper theorem. No second full upper compilation is needed for an attained existing bound. A capped deterministic witness search can stop on a validated target witness; it need not enumerate every chunk to establish the lower bound. A miss is inconclusive. This is a proposed simplification, not authorization to execute it. If a proposed smaller value needs a tighter upper bound, all chunks at that new horizon must be certified: one arbitrary chunk cannot establish the full-width upper theorem.

The proposed lower statement in D2 is correctly indexed for even D: all s < D/2 means all even times strictly below D. Include r < 2^W, D even and the explicit connection through run_toFun. Claim exactness only after the upper and lower declarations are kernel-checked in the actual composed environment; novelty relative to the current external kit has not been established by this read.

A linear bound for every width would imply Q01, but is stronger than Q01 as stated. Q01 says every finite-support row eventually has an even white time; finitely many starts at a fixed width give a finite D(W), without a linear growth rate. Correct the wording equating these statements. The claimed C-versus-Metal performance conclusion also needs the old odd/even phase/domain conversion and matched work/cost model; neither the old records.c table nor its walk-growth heuristic directly settles the proposed engine's performance.

## Source findings requiring correction

- The recurrence branches, masked death update and free-bit indexing match the inspected kit at source level. All values stay inside ALL. Reconstructing depths up to W uses only the even/free branches, so the known forced-odd second-component caveat does not invalidate that particular extraction. Finite tests of t = e remain finite evidence, not a universal theorem. An independently checked physical witness is the appropriate lower-bound gate.
- D_run always writes `D = k` and exits successfully, even if `all_dead` is false or `witness_consistent` is false. Contrary to L805, consistency is not asserted. Require explicit terminal verdicts: incomplete/live lanes give a censored maximum among eliminated lanes, not D(W); witness mismatch is a failure and stops subsequent widths. Guard absent maxima/identifiers before reconstruction. The W52 prediction check is prose only, not an enforced stop.
- tests() reports Booleans and tables without asserting their required outcomes or producing a failing exit status. T4 merely tests that a mutant changes a result; route mutated output through the actual acceptance validator so the negative is rejected. Retain the independent physical-domain comparison and literal orbit controls. Old Lean K=1 rejection is not a negative control of this new Python result pipeline.
- No executable stage/CPU/RSS/pressure/swap/disk/output supervisor is supplied. A caller's ulimit does not establish a 6 GiB simultaneous aggregate stop for four processes on this host. Four Python workers have not been agreed by the two-worker Lean pilot. Freeze concurrency, wall and cumulative CPU budgets, pressure/swap policy, owned-process cleanup, output/disk limits and no-retry behavior in the proposed source version.
