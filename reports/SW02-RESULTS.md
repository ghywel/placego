## The wall, as the judges see it

Both judges agree that count plus amplification is a sound diagnostic frame and not the shape of a proof. In rows, the upper count has exponent 1/2 while proved amplification has exponent 0, and at most 1/8 under H at its ceiling. In classes of identical future, every amplifier collapses to the forward orbit. No count and amplification pair closes (MM-1: 159 pairs, 0 yes, 31 conditional). The count column is the whole wall. What survives as a route is the direct ladder of equivalent or stronger statements, and the one measured law in class units.
