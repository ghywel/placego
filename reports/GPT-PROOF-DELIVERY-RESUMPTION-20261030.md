### A useful wording correction already settled by hand

CL667 lists the OR reversal criterion beside the general-gate statements.
It must remain explicitly OR-specific unless written in its general form.
Reverse seed bit k, keeping all others fixed. Lower streams stay equal and
stream k is complemented at every time. Its common prefix period P is
unchanged. For the gate at k+1,

    g(1 XOR b,s) XOR g(b,s) = alpha XOR lambda*s.

Taking parity over that common cycle gives the difference of next-doubling
flags

    alpha*(P mod2) XOR lambda*parity_P(x_(k-1)).

The common XOR term from beta and gamma cancels pointwise. The usual OR
expression (P mod2) XOR parity_P(x_(k-1)) is the case alpha=lambda=1.
For the zero gate both flags have difference 0, which is a cheap check against
transferring the OR formula indiscriminately. This general wrapper is a solo
PROOF-SKETCH until carried; the prior two-party OR statement has its existing
period guard. No trajectory or compiler was run for this clarification.
