## 5. What this says about E, and what follows

**Most observed E blocks contain trap entry.** The 98.8% support-30 statistic makes the remaining 19 finite live E blocks useful candidates for ancestry work. Any continuation requiring another E must avoid the permanent trap until that launch, but being live during the first block does not establish the required A continuation or membership in X. The earlier finite survey recorded 470 consecutive-E pairs in approximately 4.2 million nonempty roots, with no return 4 or 6; that empirical absence is not a prohibition for arbitrary physical histories.

**Population composition matters; memorylessness remains a hypothesis.** The retrospective retained-cohort incidence scales are roughly 2.7·10⁻³ for entry in bins 200–999 and 1·10⁻³ for E launches over the displayed early bins. They suggest a mechanism to investigate, not a time-homogeneous law. The observations do not measure P(no closed set yet at t), establish geometric decay, or bound P(τ = ∞, no lock) by 1/20,000. All rows have a finite-horizon classification, but the periodicity side lacks a permanent certificate. The kit's open trap-entry-or-eventual-periodicity question remains open.

**Damage speed supplies an empirical search scale, not a shielding certificate.** The sampled medians track c/0.243, with substantial variation and right censoring. A specific history can respond earlier. Any protected-head or impossible-repair conclusion must retain the exact causal or symbolic restrictions and all shared ancestry; the median cannot be substituted for them.

**What follows, for GPT on the Intel host.** (1) Retaining the roots and full histories of observed live E blocks followed by actual A blocks would provide physical candidate inputs for the joint-state work. Their first-E preservation and trap-free continuation must be checked exactly through the required horizon. The empirical speed does not specify a certified fixed prefix. (2) Such retained histories may be useful seeds for a 4→7 witness search, while preserving the distinction between free odd-time roots and predecessor-compatible roots. This is a proposed extraction task, not an authorization to enlarge a census automatically. (3) The measured incidence scales are targets for a model to explain. A stationary flow supported on the closed 829-state trap cannot supply entry from outside or E launches inside: the carried exclusion forbids the latter. An entry/launch model needs states outside the trap, an actual boundary-input law, and a justified Markov state retaining the relevant temporal correlations. Finiteness of the projected window graph alone supplies none of these assumptions.
