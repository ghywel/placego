**8.5 The advice split (P3-3.2). B1, B2 carried; B3, B4 carried conditional on cited theorems.** Model: the kit machine with a read-only tape depending only on k = bitLen n. B1: with the truth table as advice, decrementing the input in place costs O(1) amortised per step and moves the advice head once per decrement, so every column is computed in time C(n + 1): advised literal Problem 3 is false for every column. B2: in time T a machine sees at most 2T + 1 advice cells, hence computes at most |Γ|^(2T+1) functions on n < 2^k; the column of a uniformly random configuration is uniformly random (Rule 30 is left-permutive, so the left half maps bijectively and measure-preservingly onto the column for any fixed right half), so the chance of a hit is at most 2^(−2^(k−1)) when T ≤ 2^k/(4 log₂|Γ|) − 1; Borel–Cantelli and a countable union give the statement for almost every configuration. B3: advised time 2^(εk) for every machine at all large k is equivalent to circuit size 2^(δk) at all large k, by evaluating a circuit from the advice in time about s² and by the tableau of a machine with its advice window hard-wired (both standard quadratic simulations); the consequence P = BPP is Impagliazzo–Wigderson 1997 (cited, not opened). B4: circuit size is at most (1 + o(1))·2^k/k (Lupanov, cited, not opened), which is o(n), so no circuit bound reaches the verbal form.
