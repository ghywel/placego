## Carrying arguments and repairs

### 24: full-support finite-range Gibbs and Markov laws

**Original L-1 conclusion CARRIED, hand proof using cited established premises.**
This is a statement about shift-invariant measures on the whole binary line.
Let μ be a finite-range Gibbs measure for a real local potential h and suppose
F⁴μ=μ. F⁴ is surjective and the full shift is strongly irreducible. Kari–Taati
[Theorem41 and Corollary43](https://arxiv.org/html/1311.2319v3#S4.SS2) imply
that F⁴ conserves h. Their Proposition37 says its integral is unchanged under
F⁴ for every shift-invariant measure. This includes every periodic-row measure.

Fix h's word length m>0. Put an integer linear equation on h for every directed
cycle of the order-(m+7) binary de Bruijn graph: the sum of the new h values
minus the old h values is zero. Cycles correspond to periodic input rows;
`Word.image_eq_four` and `Word.crop` give exactly that difference. Thus h lies
in this kernel. There are finitely many vertices and edges, so the cycle
equations have a finite integer basis; equivalently choose any finite basis
of their row space. Gaussian elimination gives a rational basis of the kernel.
Write h as a real linear combination of rational kernel vectors and clear
denominators separately in each basis vector.

The actual kit theorem
`Rule30LocalConservation.cyclic_conserved_iff_shift_coboundary` applies to
each resulting integer density at this same m. It writes it as a constant
plus B(v.tail)−B(v.dropLast). Recombine the finitely many B's with the same
real coefficients. Hence h itself is a constant plus a real local shift
coboundary. Its integral is the same constant under every shift-invariant
measure, so its equilibrium measures are exactly the measures of maximal
shift entropy. On the binary full shift the unique such measure is fair
Bernoulli. In one dimension the finite-range Gibbs/equilibrium identification
applies; a full-support finite-order stationary Markov law uses the finite
real potential given by log transition probabilities. This proves the stated
exclusion under F⁴ and therefore under F.

This construction avoids silently extending the kit's integer **local-current**
theorem to arbitrary real currents: it first uses the **cyclic** version and
finite-dimensional rational linear algebra. No strong transitivity hypothesis
is asserted. Hard constraints, non-full-support periodic/SFT measures,
infinite-range potentials and the zero-boundary half-row frame are outside
the conclusion. In particular GC2828's nonuniform positive-entropy σ,F measure
is no counterexample to this full-support finite-range result.

### 59: distance is exactly the first failed constraint

**R-12's original argument CARRIED.** Write the t-th low-bit reading in the
zero-boundary frame as a_t(Y)=Y_t XOR P_t(Y_0,...,Y_(t−1)); this is the usual
triangular left-permutive reconstruction. K imposes a_t=1 at every even t;
odd positions are free. If t is the first failing even index, no point of K
can agree with Y through bit t, since that would preserve the failed reading.
Conversely keep all bits below t, flip bit t, then choose every later even
bit recursively to meet its constraint, leaving odd bits arbitrary. This
produces a point of K first differing at t. With |2|₂=1/2 the distance is
exactly2^(−t). If there is no failed index, Y∈K and the distance is0.

The code's lifetime unit must be declared: a double-step index q gives t=2q.
This is a distance to the entire closed survivor set, not to a previously
selected point of it, and does not prove that finite rows miss K.

### 67: the residue witness, including the square's separate obligation

The one-tick numeric map in L-4 is
F(n)=(n>>1) XOR (n OR 2n). **The original non-GCF/non-polyhedral-PAF
argument is CARRIED for F.** Ben-Amram's
[Definitions3–4](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/storage/00lipics/lipics-vol020-stacs2013/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.514/LIPIcs.STACS.2013.514.pdf)
specify finite rational polyhedral pieces, or one affine formula on each
residue modulo a fixed m. In one dimension finite polyhedral pieces force
one affine formula on the sufficiently positive ray. Arbitrary subsets are
not being called polyhedral pieces here.

For any m=2^e q with q odd, fix an order o with2^o=1 mod q (o=1 for q=1).
Choose k→∞ through multiples of o, with k≥e and above the fixed residue r.
Let S be q isolated one-bits at positions k,k+8o,...,k+8o(q−1), and let
D=2^K(2^K−1), with K=k+8oq. Both are0 modulo m. On these separated blocks,

```math
F(r+S)=F(r)+7S/2,
F(r+D)=F(r)+3\,2^{2K-1}+2^{K-1}.
```

Thus on the same residue class r the two ratios tend to7/2 and3/2,
contradicting one affine coefficient. The original run's invalid shortcut
using just2^k for odd m is not used; q sparse bits repair it.

**Optional extension, new PROOF-SKETCH:** for the unguarded square G=F²,
compute rather than assume closure failure under composition:

```math
G(r+S)=G(r)+25S/4,
G(r+D)=G(r)+13\,2^{2K-2}+7\,2^{K-2}.
```

One isolated bit becomes three adjacent bits under F; a second application
gives the25/4 factor. A long one-block first becomes its two high bits and
one low bit; those separated pieces give the13/4 limit under G. The fixed
control checks30 exact cases, including both formulas. The same all-m
argument would exclude G from these classes, but this newly written
extension is not labelled two-party before another worker reads it.
This is not a classification of G restricted to K, an invariant/guarded
subdomain, nor a barrier to conjugacy or higher-dimensional encodings.

### 91: why the proposed method closure is too broad

Read Berzai's actual remarks `fr:tropical`, `fr:rational`, `fr:exists`, and
the §24 table in `RULE30_PAPER.tex` at d9cb3d57b28f5a713a4ac28c11f5723cbe14d6a4.
The numerical statements remain the author's measurements, not new replays.

For any sequence M, a(d,e)=M_(d+e)−M_d obeys
a(d,e+f)=a(d,e)+a(d+e,f). This identity alone is satisfied by sequences with
nonconvergent M_d/d (use successively much longer blocks of increments1
and2). It therefore supplies no existence theorem. It also does not forbid
one: if the increments are an integrable observable along a stationary
ergodic system and this particular orbit is generic, Birkhoff applies.
A deterministic sequence can be generic for a non-Dirac stationary measure;
only demanding stationarity of the single Dirac mass forces a fixed word.

The restart envelope sup_d(M_(d+e)−M_d) is subadditive **if finite**.
Its Fekete limit would give an envelope bound, not automatically the actual
orbit's speed; identifying the two would need another argument.
Observed jumps close to the tested periods, together with unbounded periods,
do not prove its one-step supremum infinite: a uniform family of large
actual jumps must be proved. Likewise measured growth of Chebyshev
discrepancy does not prove unbounded discrepancy. If unboundedness were
proved it would exclude the specified autonomous irreducible finite
max-plus model; periodic backgrounds alone leave the transient gate as an
external input. It does not exclude every driven finite-state model.

Two concrete repairs survive: establish a stationary integrable coding and
genericity for the actual front, or establish deterministic almost-
subadditivity with a summably small error. No such hypothesis is proved
here. The additive identity and finite measurements are retained; the
blanket Kingman/Fekete/blocker/max-plus closure is not carried.
