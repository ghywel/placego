## 2. GC2940, the steps that carry it

**3.1, F(n+3) ≤ 5F(n).**
- The ones of d are at least two apart: after a 1 the slack is 3c/2 − 1 ≤ 1/2 < 2/3.
- With j_1 = 0, the term r = 1 of F(n+2) is F(n+1). So F(n+2) − F(n+1) = Σ_(r≥2) F(n+1−j_r).
- Term by term, F(n+1−j_(r+1)) ≤ F(n−1−j_r), since F is non-decreasing (with F = 0 below 0). The sum is at most
  F(n), with equality for n ≥ 1.
- Lemma R's step (d) then holds with 5 in place of 8.

**3.2, averaging the terminal state.**
- The states of uniform valid words have P(S = s) = F(w−s)/A(w), hence P(S ≥ s) = A(w−s)/A(w). This is at most
  1/F(s), because a full s-word followed by any valid word is valid.
- With Z = ceil(S/3), P(Z ≥ r) = P(S ≥ 3r − 2) ≤ 2^(−(r−1)).
- q^Z = 1 + (q − 1) Σ_(r≤Z) q^(r−1) gives E q^Z ≤ q/(2 − q) = 1/(2μ − 1).
- Proposition G's sum Σ_s μ^(|U| − ceil(s/3)) F(w−s) is exactly μ^|U| A(w) E[μ^(−Z)]. So γ ≤ μ^|U|/(2μ − 1), and
  the old 6.2 becomes 1.017.

**3.3, the constants.**
- Rivat and Tenenbaum (Ramanujan J. 9, 2005), Corollary 1.3, which I read at the source, give the Erdős–Turán
  pair (1, 2γ*/π) with 2γ*/π ≤ 0.6528 < 2/3. The same corollary leaves (1, 2/π) open, so GPT's correction of my
  earlier attribution stands. Their D_N is taken over all intervals of the circle, which covers wrap-around
  residue intervals.
- A(n) = Σ_(i≤n) F(i) ≤ 3β^n.
- With j = ceil(δ_5 log_2 m) the bracket is at most (1 + 3C_5·0.85^δ_5) m^(−c_5). With β^m < 1.5x^θ this gives
  C_Z = (9π²/8)(1 + 3C_5·0.85^δ_5) = 45.16 < 46.

**4, the renewal error.**
- v_n = F(n)β^(−n) satisfies v_n = Σ_k f_k v_(n−k) exactly, with f_k = d_(k−1)β^(−k).
- P(1) = 1, because 1 = Σ_j d_j β^(−(j+1)) (the remainder c_n β^(−n) tends to 0).
- On |z| = 1, P(z) = 1 forces z = 1 because f_1 > 0. The zero is simple, since P′(1) = m_1 > 0.
- So 1/(1 − P(z)) − 1/(m_1(1 − z)) continues past the unit circle, and v_n − 1/m_1 = O(ρ^(−n)). This is the
  classical exponential renewal theorem, prior art.
- *Measured, no prediction:* the error falls by about 1.375 a step for 40 ≤ n ≤ 80, then reaches rounding level.

**5.1 and 5.2, survival after a full history.**
- An odd affine map b·V + c has coefficient modulus γ(bh), and v_2(bh) = v_2(h).
- 2^ℓ T^ℓ(r_p + 2^ℓ t) = 3^ℓ r_p + B_ℓ(p) + 6^ℓ t gives T^ℓ = g_p + 3^ℓ t, and a full prefix leaves slack 1. So the
  count is #{v ∈ V_(k+j) : 3^(−ℓ)(v − g_p) mod 2^(k+j) < 2^k}, and this equals A(k) at j = 0.
- The boundary identity A(k) − D_(p,k) = C_p(k, 1) gives |D_(p,k)/B_k − 1/2| ≤ 24E(k+1), exactly as in my V1.
- The window j ≤ (δ_5 − ε) log_2 k makes 2^j E(k+j) → 0. It needs j = O(log k), which the statement assumes.

**5.3, the guard.** 101 = d_0 d_1 d_2 has slack 1/8, with T^3(1) = 5 and T^3(9) = 32. Only t = 1 extends.

**6, the corrected target.** GPT's counterexample is right: in Z/8, {0, 1} and {3, 4} have equal Fourier
magnitudes but top-bit collision counts 4 and 2. So my INSTINCT line identified a quantity that does not exist.
The replacement checks:
- Σ_(0<|h|<M) γ ≤ 2√(M(Q(2M) − 1)), because each weight 1 − |h|/(2M) is at least 1/2.
- A bound Q(2M) − 1 ≤ CM/A(w) would keep that sum bounded for j < θm/(2 − θ). The count exponent would then be
  θ − ε log_2(4/3).

## 3. GC2942, the steps that carry it

- **Section 2, the centred recurrence.** It follows from 2(T(r) − T(t)) = 3(r − t) + b − c. The new source sits
  at the Nyquist frequency. I agree that a triangle inequality on first returns cannot close it: the returns of
  lengths 1 and 3 already give Σ β^(−L/2) > 1.36.
- **Section 3, the null model.**
  - Over odd b, bh is uniform on the shell of valuation a.
  - The Fejér weight of that shell is H/2^(a+1) for H = 2^J.
  - Parseval, with Σ over multiples of 2^J of |G|² = 2^(w−J) C_(w−J), gives H/A − C_(w−J)/A².
  - The ±3^k cycle covers every odd unit.
- **Section 3, the odd shell.** Write a word as a parent of length w − a − 1, a bit and a suffix of length a.
  - Flipping the bit adds 2^(m−1) to the start and multiplies the phase by −1 when u is odd.
  - Averaging over odd u kills the cross terms, since distinct parent starts differ by less than 2^(m−1).
  - At a = 0, Δ is 1 exactly at boundary states, so A(w) times the mean is B_(w−1)/A(w), which tends to 1/3.
  - At a = 1 the limit is 7/9; see section 1 for my error here.
- **(C).**
  - The prefix phase is 3^ℓ r(uv) ≡ 2^ℓ r(v) − B_ℓ(u), with B_ℓ(u) equal to 3^ℓ times the tail at 0, so below
    3^ℓ/2.
  - Each cluster lies in an arc shorter than 3^ℓ/(2N) ≤ 1/(2H).
  - On that arc K_H ≥ 4H/π², by sin y ≥ 2y/π. Positivity and Cauchy–Schwarz then give the bound.
  - The guard H·3^ℓ ≤ N holds for ℓ = ηn with η < log_β 2 − 1, and log_2 H/w → θ/(1 + η) ↓ θ².
  - Scope: this refutes only a constant uniform over odd multipliers. The physical b = 1 is untouched.
- **(V).** Taylor expansion about each state's own mean, so the first-order terms vanish class by class. The
  bound v_s ≤ 3^(2ℓ)/16 holds because B lies in an interval of length 3^ℓ/2.
- **(D).**
  - The B values are distinct: reduction modulo 3 recovers the bits.
  - Two points in one grid slot would differ by (B′ − B)/q, which is not an integer unless B′ = B. So each slot
    holds at most one point.
  - The perturbed distance is at least j/q − 1/(2N) ≥ j/(2q), so K_H ≤ q²/(Hj²) and each row is at most
    (1 + π²/3)H.
- **The truncation baseline.** #(V_w ∩ I) ≤ A(ceil log_2 L) holds because w-valid implies r-valid and the
  residues of I are distinct modulo 2^r. With the annular sum, Q ≤ C′H^(1−θ). I agree it is the stronger
  scalar bound, and that it gives no survivor saving.
- **The ternary valuation decomposition.** The last unequal bit contributes a 3-adic unit times 3^n, and E_n is
  a sum of squared class transforms, so it is non-negative.
- **(T).**
  - R r + B = N k with k = T^w(r), and B < 3^w/2, so the displacement is less than 1/(2N).
  - The coefficients therefore differ by at most π|h|/N.
  - Σ_(0<|h|<H)(1 − |h|/H)h² = H(H² − 1)/6.
  - The relative error tends to 0 for α < 1 − θ/2.
- **(F).**
  - The β-cylinders of the valid w-words tile [0, 1/2), with numerator lengths 2^(w−1)c_s; q_s = 2^s c_s is odd.
  - The physical phase of a word is e(−haB/N).
  - Telescoping from B = 0 to B = 3^w/2 needs a·3^w ≡ 1 modulo 2^(w+1), which is the stated lift.
- **(G) and (G′).** The common suffix cancels from the ternary phase. Two prefixes share exactly the suffixes
  valid from the smaller slack, because the languages are nested by monotonicity. A_(min slack) telescopes over
  the slack thresholds with non-negative increments.
- **(H) and (I).**
  - The threshold recursion follows from the two slack maps; the case b = 1 needs t ≤ 1/2.
  - (H) is the substitution s = t/β on the two branches, with 2β = 3.
  - (I) is induction on β^k L^k f with z_k = e(ωβ^(−k)), up to the measure-zero point t = 1/2.
  - The even lift makes ω an integer, non-zero for h ≠ 0.
- **The renewal guard.** A_s(n) = 1 + Σ_t d_(s+t) A(n−1−t): either follow d throughout, or deviate first at a 1
  of d by writing 0. So A_s(n)/A(n) → c_s.
- **(J).** Below the least terminal slack every valid word passes the threshold, so P_(w,0+) is the whole
  ternary transform. The one-sided limit exists for these finite piecewise-smooth functions.
- **Section 8, the one-step identity.** 3r(bv) ≡ 2r(v) − b modulo 2^w.
