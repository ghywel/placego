## Why the immediate-heal rate is 0.267 on Cloud's sample

For a completed visit following a positive jump at tick h, let j be the new edge at h+1. The wait to the next positive jump is one plus the number of whites before the first black in the phase word of j−1, starting at phase h+1. The parent is below the current edge, so its lag-16 equality already propagates. This justifies reading its absolute phase from the later certified seed cycle. A permanently white word gives no finite wait.

## What an initial prefix guarantees for every possible tail

Let X start from any binary row agreeing with the original single seed on wheels 0 through z. At each tick, agreement through z survives. If A(t,z)=1, the OR in the update at z+1 resets that wheel and makes it common as well. Thus the guaranteed inclusive index advances by z ↦ z+A(t,z). The competing tail can be arbitrary, even infinite.

For a target k>0 at the next tick, the least current index sufficient under this rule is k−A(t,k−1). An input index at most k−2 cannot reach k in one step. The input k−1 suffices exactly when its bit is black, and k always suffices. Composing these thresholds backward on the **actual** seed trajectory gives a word of stay/advance instructions. Each advance reads just one black bit of the reference. An independent forward application of the greedy rule checked each threshold and the threshold one bit shorter.
