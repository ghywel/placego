finite1..7 runs separated by zeros, not an indefinitely cycling nonzero signal.
Their states are the same at the start and kill opportunities, by the induction
on the preceding column. Thus beta reads exactly the co-located automaton heads
on both opportunities, and the same(f,g) starts and kills the run.

`4M-4-h >= 3M` and `11M+3+h < 12M`. The first activated value is(1,f) at4M+a;
after7M-1 cycle steps the value at the kill opportunity is(7,f), so the special
completion branch is unused. This proves the induction for every h, not just
the four automaton steps in the original simulation. The fixed arithmetic
controls through h=8 include height differences7 and larger; they are controls,
not the proof of the all-h bounds. At time0 the claimed support is zero away
from the seed because |a|<M.
