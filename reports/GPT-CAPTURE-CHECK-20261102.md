### What this changes about capture and information

The389-state uncertainty is invisible to the front's next move once we know its
phase. It is not invisible to the entire row: intermediate windows may branch,
and older material is still present. At the four reset phases even the window
uncertainty disappears. Thus the finite certificate gives a precise, observable
form of forgetting, with its domain stated explicitly: **front motion inside
S_E**. It does not make Rule30 reversible or recover discarded ancestors.

A useful equivalent target is now one **gateway**, say `(23,76)` at depth>=12.
For a bounded-front candidate, showing eventual entry to S_E at depth>=12
implies that gateway is reached later at depth>=12: if its first occurrence
were below12, repeat the52-cycle, gaining68. Conversely the gateway itself is
in S_E, so entry there suffices. Window76 means the eight mismatch bits,
nearest to farthest behind the front, are `00110010`. This replaces389 possible
entrance states by one exact return target, without claiming that its hitting
time is bounded or that every finite prefix hits it. Local's fuel problem is
still the missing universal entry argument.

Adversarial scope checks: phase membership alone is insufficient; the window
must lie in that phase's certified fibre. A52-periodic advance word does not
force a52-periodic whole wake. Four hundred successful rows do not imply all
rows. The W8 bad-core witnesses close the tested arbitrary-input abstraction;
without a separate argument they do not prove impossibility of every larger-W

`max(K+8,12) <= R <= max(K+8,12)+1023`

*Proof.* The prescribed prefix lies entirely at depths<=K−1<=R−9. There is
exactly one mismatch at or beyond depthK, namely at R, so R is the deepest

`195*(-21)=1 (mod1024)`, the stated congruence gives
`theta+195R=882 (mod1024)`. Therefore the initial state is exactly `(882,0)`,
which is in the independently checked S_E, and R>=12. Apply GC2386. ∎

This gives **every prescribed finite prefix** a captured completion by a single

K+1031. For each fixed K,theta,R it covers all2^K prefixes at once. There is no
sampling premise. In particular capturing finite-prefix rows are dense in the
prefix topology: every specification of finitely many shallow cells has such a
completion. This is a standard topology/counting consequence, not a novelty
claim or a statement that capture is probable, generic, or universal.

**Cheap objection settled:** this construction is allowed to alter the original
row farther ahead. It does not prove capture for the unmodified row. A universal
capture proof would need the original evolution to create a certified local
state by itself. The one-flip construction identifies a particularly simple
sufficient state to look for, not a necessary state for capture. Membership in S_E does not force every occurrence of phase882 to have
window0: its certified fibre also contains other windows. No assertion that
every path hits `(882,0)` is made; unlike the four singleton fibres, this is
not a forced reset.

P1024 tail/alignment, the same front R>=12 and any two certified windows at the
same phase have identical future front positions. Both follow the same
projected phase clock. Their mutual mismatch is always strictly behind that
front; at each singleton reset it is at least nine cells behind. They need not
become identical, nor does their separation from the front have to grow. In
particular arbitrary changes confined behind the eight-cell window of the
one-flip marker cannot change its future front trajectory. This is a precise
shielding statement for an observable, despite continued information in the
