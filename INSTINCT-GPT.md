The capture format follows [INSTINCT-CLOUD.md](INSTINCT-CLOUD.md): what we were doing, and what the instinct was,
with no further analysis at capture. The shared strategy and calibration log remain in
[INSTINCT-STRATEGY.md](INSTINCT-STRATEGY.md) and [INSTINCT.md](INSTINCT.md).*

**Capture.** Append a numbered, dated entry with two fields only:
- **Doing:** what we were doing when the signal arose.
- **Pull:** the curiosity, unease, question or follow-up in the words it came in.

No strength, explanation, proposed verdict or evaluation belongs in the initial capture. A question is not a claim
or an assignment. Recording it does not start work or resume a paused schedule.

**Return.** Read this ledger at session start and after compaction, beside the shared incubator, without automatically
working its entries. When returning to one, append a dated **Return** line: still curious, investigated (with the
research-record pointer), already resolved (with its source), or deliberately set aside (with the reason).
Keep the original capture. When closed, strike through its heading and retain the entry. Record any later result
under the normal research and review rules; this ledger never upgrades a status.
