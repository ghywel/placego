## How to use this page

- Work the blocks in order, unless the owner says otherwise. One block at a time, except where a row says it can run beside another.
- **To start a block:** say so in the ledger, set its status here to DOING, and give it a row on the board (PERIOD-TWO §6.5). Write the prediction, counterfactual and `Record searched:` line first (AGENTS §3).
- **To close a block:** set its status to DONE here, with the date and ledger entry, mark its board row in the same commit, update the door's verdict in `SW02-DOORS.md`, tick what it clears in the backlog, and add a line to NOW. Then the next block starts.
- **Closing a block also means (CL762):** tick or annotate every backlog item it settles, update its door's verdict (or add a dated amendment at the top of `SW02-DOORS.md`), and update the harvest ledger row. Then a short harvest pass: name anything the block made submittable.
- A finished result that could be submitted goes into the submission queue, not into a block. A new idea goes into the backlog, and only becomes a block on the owner's word.
- Search `rule30-open`, Berzai and the kit before calling anything new. Of 54 claims checked in SW-02, only 3 were not found.
